## What is openo11y.dev?

Observability is more than dashboards and alerts. It's the socio-technical practice of understanding systems through their outputs — and then acting on that understanding. **openo11y.dev** is an open-source practitioner's guide covering the full scope of observability: instrumenting technical systems, ethically measuring human systems, observing AI systems, and honing the practice that connects them all.

> **telemetry + analysis = observability**
>
> — *Learning OpenTelemetry*, Ted Young & Austin Parker

This site is opinionated. It's built on the belief that observability is evolving — from siloed signal types toward braided, inferable telemetry that shortens feedback loops and deepens understanding. [OpenTelemetry](https://opentelemetry.io) is the standard at the center of that evolution.
